# Working on assam-heritage-ai-art

Gallery of 58 AI-generated artworks of Assamese heritage. Part of AxomRank: see README.md for the overview and roadmap.

## Layout

- `images/`: original artworks (never rename or re-encode)
- `data/gallery.json`: original metadata (unchanged)
- `catalog.json`, `catalog.js`, `thumbnails/`: generated by `build_gallery.py`

## Commands

- `python build_gallery.py` (checks checksums, rebuilds thumbnails and the catalogue)

## Rules

- Keep artwork ids stable, because they are used as direct links
- Always label the images as AI interpretations, not historical likenesses
- Do not delete duplicates automatically. Use `duplicate-review.html`
- Commit as the `axomrank` account, in small reviewable commits.
